FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

How can the chargers and installation be free for condos?

SyncEV keeps ownership of the hardware and earns its margin on the charging service sold to drivers. The building gets the infrastructure at no cost and still receives $0.10 for every kWh consumed.

Who pays the electricity?

We do. Each station is metered and the electricity is billed to SyncEV, never to the building owner. We then bill the drivers directly.

Who do drivers call when something goes wrong?

Us. Customer service is part of every offer, so property managers and owners are never the first line of support.

Can I buy a charger for my house?

Yes. Homeowners can buy online or on Amazon, and available subsidies can cover most of the cost. See the homeowner offer.

Which regions do you serve?

We currently operate across Québec, and our partner electrician network is opening region by region. Check coverage.
