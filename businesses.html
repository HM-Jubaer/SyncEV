Commercial FAQ

Before You Book

Why is there no price on this page?

Because commercial sites vary a lot. The number of stations, the electrical work and whether installation can be covered all depend on your expected volume. Fifteen minutes on a call gives you a real answer instead of a vague range.

Is installation actually free?

It can be, where the charging volume supports it — it isn't automatic. The scoping call tells you which side of that line your site falls on.

Do we set the price drivers pay?

Driver pricing is managed by SyncEV so it stays consistent across the network. Your revenue is the $0.10 per kWh share.

What should we prepare for the call?

A rough parking count, whether the lot is public or private, and your electrical panel capacity if you know it. If not, we'll work it out together.
