Electrician FAQ

What Partners Ask Us

Why only one electrician per city?

So that when a building in your city needs something, there's no confusion about whose job it is — it's yours. It also means the leads we generate in your zone go to you instead of being split six ways.

Why can't I order online right away?

Because partner access isn't public. We verify that you're a licensed electrician first; after that your account unlocks and ordering is self-serve.

Does the $10 per month apply to house calls?

No. The partner offer covers stations installed and activated in multi-unit buildings only.

My region isn't open. Is that permanent?

Not at all — we open regions progressively. Join the waitlist and you'll get first refusal when yours opens.
