Condo FAQ

Questions Boards Ask Us

Does the syndicate pay anything at all?

No. Installation, hardware and the electricity bill are all covered by SyncEV. The building receives $0.10 per kWh consumed.

Who owns the chargers?

SyncEV. They remain our property, which is why there is no capital cost and no maintenance liability for the building.

How is the electricity measured and billed?

Each station meters its own consumption. The electricity is billed to SyncEV, and we bill the drivers — the building's own Hydro-Québec bill is untouched.

What happens if a resident has a problem at 9 p.m.?

They contact SyncEV support directly. Property managers are never the first line of support and never have to dispatch anyone.

How many stations will our building get?

That's what the free assessment determines. We size the installation to the number of EV drivers in the building and leave room to expand.
