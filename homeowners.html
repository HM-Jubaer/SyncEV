The Écorecharge Grant

How The $600 Grant Works

The Government of Québec's Écorecharge program offers EV owners a $600 grant for the purchase and installation of a home charging station. You apply after your station is installed.

  • 1Own an electric vehicleYou need to own an EV when you submit your application.
  • 2Buy an eligible chargerThe station must be on the eligible list and cost more than $600 before taxes.
  • 3Have it installed by an electricianYou'll need proof of installation for your application.
  • 4Apply onlineSubmit your Écorecharge application once the station is installed.

Homeowner FAQ

Common Questions

Is the charger really free?

Not literally, and we'd rather be straight about it. The Écorecharge grant pays $600 toward the station and its installation, and the $0.10 per kWh cash back keeps coming after that. Together they cover a large part of the cost.

Who applies for the grant?

You do, online through the Government of Québec's Écorecharge program, once your station is installed. We provide the invoice and product documents you need.

How do I receive the $0.10 per kWh?

Your station meters its consumption through the SyncEV app, and the cash back is calculated from that metered total.

Can I buy it on Amazon instead?

Yes — same product, same warranty. Use whichever checkout you prefer.

I live in a condo. Is this for me?

Condo buildings have their own offer with free chargers and free installation. See the condo offer or share it with your syndicate.
